This project evolves a general data-analysis harness under analysis-research-sequence@1.
Python GEPA owns research decisions; the Project runtime owns budgets and promotion.
One rollout is one experiment. One evaluation cell is a budgeted sequence of
experiments from a frozen shared checkpoint. task_inspect supplies the total and
remaining experiment budget. All experiments and methods use the same held-out
validation set, frozen before research. Target may choose any internal training
strategy, including CV or no CV, using only its supplied training rows.

Change only harness prompts, skills, and workflows/research/{policy.py,memory.py,policy.json}.
Target generates solution code at runtime; never put Titanic features, passenger IDs,
model solutions, submission rows, or answers into the harness. Fixed drivers, tools,
budgets, scorer, dependencies and model access are not editable candidates.

Inspect authorized seed research: hypothesis, attempted change, internal validation,
negative evidence, errors, source selection and actual final delivery. State one
falsifiable mechanism prediction before editing. An unchanged tool-success rate does
not imply the research or selection policy is good. Use a controlled, general change.
Never use a later checkpoint or more experiments for one side of a comparison.

The default objective is actual final quality with valid_output >= 1. Research curves,
audit oracle and selection loss are diagnostics; they cannot replace delivery or be copied
into Target history. Shared-set promotion means development champion only. A single
Titanic sequence per harness is not independent replication or transfer evidence.

Model tokens and requests are usage-only under the user's chosen profile. Keep the
admitted experiment count and CPU, wall, memory, tools and output limits. Do not claim a
hard token budget, complete retry accounting or known monetary cost without evidence.
